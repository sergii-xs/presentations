L'Anse aux Meadows

The Viking Discovery

  • 🏛️ UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1978
  • 📅 Precisely dated to 1021 CE through tree-ring analysis
  • 🏘️ Eight timber-framed turf buildings housed 30-160 Norse settlers
  • 🔨 First iron production in North America
  • 🌰 Butternut artifacts prove exploration reached New Brunswick
  • ⏰ Predates Columbus by 471 years

"Where North America's European story truly begins"

The Newfoundland English

16 distinct dialects - the world's greatest concentration!

  • "What are ya at?" (or "Whaddayat?") which means "How are you?" or "What are you doing?"
  • "Landwash" - the shoreline between high and low tide
  • "Mauzy" - damp, foggy, drizzly weather
  • "Stunned as me arse" - very foolish
  • "Long may your big jib draw" - may you have good fortune
  • "Stay where you're to 'til I comes where you're at" - wait there for me

The Screech-In Ceremony

Becoming an Honorary Newfoundlander

  1. 🥃 Down a shot of Screech (40% Jamaican rum)
  2. 🐟 Kiss a cod fish (frozen is acceptable!)
  3. 🥩 Eat "Newfoundland Steak" (a slice of bologna)
  4. 📜 Receive your certificate as an honorary Newfoundlander

Created in 1974, now performed over 1 million times!

Puffin Capital of North America

Witless Bay Ecological Reserve

  • 🐧 260,000+ breeding pairs of Atlantic Puffins
  • 🐦 4 million seabirds total in summer
  • 🐟 Puffins can carry 61 fish in their beaks
  • 🏊 Dive up to 200 feet deep for food
  • ❤️ Mate for life and return to same burrow
  • 🏝️ North America's largest Atlantic Puffin colony

Peak viewing: mid-June to mid-July

From Cod to Oil

Economic Revolution

The Cod Collapse (1992)

  • 📉 500-year-old fishery ends
  • 👥 37,000 jobs lost (12% of workforce)
  • 🏘️ Outport communities devastated
  • 🔄 2024: Limited reopening after 32 years

The Oil Boom

  • ⛽ $46 billion (1997-2007)
  • 🛢️ 35% of provincial GDP
  • 💰 Ended equalization payments
  • 🏗️ 4 major platforms operating
From "have-not" to "have" province in one generation

The Reluctant Canadian

Path to Confederation

1907-1934: Independent Dominion equal to Canada
1934-1949: British-appointed commission government
1948: Two referendums on future
52.3% vs 47.7%: Narrowest margin for confederation
March 31, 1949: Becomes Canada's 10th province
